About me

Marco Lau

Hello! I'm a second-year Electrical & Electronics Engineering student at the University of Auckland. I'm early in the degree, but I haven't waited to start building - I'd rather learn a concept and then go test it on real hardware than leave it on paper.

I'm interested in everything on the electrical side of robotics: circuit design, sensors, embedded firmware, electronics, control systems, and AI and machine learning. Put simply, I think robots are awesome, and these are the pieces that make one work: the circuits that power it, the sensors that let it perceive, the firmware that runs it, the control loops that keep it stable, and the learning that makes it smarter. I like the theory as much as the hands-on side, and nothing beats watching the two finally meet in something that moves.

Current focus

  • PCB designing using Altium & KiCad
  • Python for automation and robotics
  • 3D Printing side hustle
